Simulation notes: the monthly withdrawal is taken directly from the portfolio (inflation-indexed in fixed-$ mode). Pension income pays at the pre-reduction rate until the "Pension Reduces At" age, then the reduced rate, both compounding by the pension COLA. Social Security starts at its start age and is treated as flat (no COLA). Pension and Social Security are separate income paid on top of the portfolio withdrawal — they are never added to the portfolio — so total spending equals withdrawal + pension + Social Security. In Projection mode a single fixed return is applied every year; Monte Carlo mode draws independent annual returns from a normal distribution centered on the expected return with standard deviation equal to the volatility input (clipped to −60%/+90%), reruns the projection for every path, and shows the survival rate plus 10th/50th/90th percentile balance bands. Interest compounds monthly or annually per your selection. Rates are nominal gross returns — subtract your effective tax drag to model after-tax performance. All values in USD.
